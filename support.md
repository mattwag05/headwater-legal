# Headwater Support

Headwater is an educational preventive-health app for iPhone. This page is the app's official support contact — it is also the Support URL listed in the App Store.

## Contact

- **Email:** worrier.last0f@icloud.com
- We aim to respond within 2 business days.

When writing in, include your iOS version and app version (shown at the bottom of Profile → Legal & Disclaimers).

## Frequently asked questions

**Who makes Headwater?**
Headwater is created and clinically reviewed by Matthew John Wagner, a board-certified family medicine physician licensed in New York and New Jersey. It is a personal wellness educational tool, not clinical decision support — see the [Medical Disclaimer](/headwater-legal/disclaimer/).

**Where is my data stored?**
Entirely on your iPhone. Headwater has no accounts and no servers, and makes no network requests. See the [Privacy Policy](/headwater-legal/privacy/).

**How do I delete my data?**
Profile → Delete All Data, or delete the app. Details: [Deleting Your Data](/headwater-legal/data-deletion/).

**How do I restore my Premium purchase on a new phone?**
Open the Plan tab, tap the **Doctor report** button at the top right to open the purchase sheet, then tap **Restore Purchases**. It is a one-time purchase tied to your Apple ID, so you never pay twice.

**Why can't I get a refund from you directly?**
Purchases are processed by Apple. Request refunds at [reportaproblem.apple.com](https://reportaproblem.apple.com).

**Why does the app ask about Apple Health?**
If you allow it, Headwater reads sleep, activity, and heart data to pre-fill your daily habit logs. It's optional, read-only, and the data stays on your device. You can revoke access in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Health → Headwater.

**Is Headwater medical advice?**
No. Headwater is an educational tool — it shows what published guidelines say about situations like yours. Always discuss health decisions with a doctor or other qualified clinician. See the [Medical Disclaimer](/headwater-legal/disclaimer/).

**Something in the content looks wrong or outdated.**
Please tell us — evidence content cites its sources, and we want corrections. Email the item's title and the issue to worrier.last0f@icloud.com.
